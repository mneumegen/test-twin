      - heading: Formulation & Capabilities
        items:
          - title: What is a soap finisher?
            contentSections:
              - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
                text: Twincraft Skincare is a soap finisher, which means that we do not manufacture soap base in-house. Instead, we partner with the world's top suppliers to use a pre-saponified base in the form of soap pellets, which become the foundation of our finished goods. [Read all about our manufacturing process here!](/learning-lab/what-is-triple-milled-bar-soap)
          - title: Extruded soap vs. poured soap, what’s the difference?
            contentSections:
              - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
                text: Many small-scale soap makers and at-home crafters are familiar with the process of making soap by pouring liquid soap into a mold and letting it set and harden before use. Poured soap allows for unique visual aesthetics to be achieved, but this process is very labor intensive and difficult to scale up. Our process for finishing and extruding bar soap is highly efficient and produces consistent bars that last much longer than a poured version.

            contentSections:
              - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text

            contentSections:

          - title: What is a die?
            contentSections:
              - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
                text: A die is the mold that presses your bar soap into its final shape. We offer a large library of dies in all different shapes and sizes.
          - title: Can I make a custom die?
            contentSections:
              - _component: building-blocks/core-elements/text
                text: Yes you can! For customers looking to customize a die, we have two options – custom die inserts or custom built dies. Inserts can be added to our existing dies and allow you to press your logo or an image of your choice into a bar. Custom built dies can be made in an exact shape and size that you want, but it takes longer and is more expensive than a die insert.
          - title: What are lot codes?
            contentSections:
