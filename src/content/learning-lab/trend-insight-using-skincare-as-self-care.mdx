Is your brand looking to offer consumers a way to elevate their skincare experience?

It’s no surprise that wellness trends are on the rise – for the past few years, consumers have been increasingly prioritizing wellness, and that includes finding ways to use skincare to support their self-care routines.

So, what exactly is self-care?

At the basis of self-care is a mentality shift – prioritizing “me-time” and actively choosing to spend time on self-care routines that have a positive effect on the mind and body. For example, self-care might include at-home skincare treatments, fragrance therapy, or thoughtful aesthetic choices.

At-home treatments are a popular way to incorporate skincare products into self-care routines. Face masks have traditionally offered consumers a way to take a break from the demands of daily life to relax and indulge in self-care. While face masks remain popular amidst the recent wellness boom, consumers are seeking out more at-home options to enhance their self-care routines, including body, scalp, and hair care products.

Fragrances and aesthetics also tie into self-care by offering an easy way to connect with your emotions and enhance your mood. For instance, citrus fragrances are known to be uplifting and energizing, while warmer fragrances like coconut and vanilla help promote relaxation and comfort. Even the colors of your skincare products can impact your emotions. Bright, warm colors like pink and yellow impart a sense of playfulness, enthusiasm, and positivity. Cooler colors like blue and green give a sense of trust, harmony, and growth.
