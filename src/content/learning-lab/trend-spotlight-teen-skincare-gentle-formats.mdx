For the last few years, we've been seeing the use of multistep skincare routines, often including harsh active ingredients, trend younger and younger. The American Academy of Pediatrics noted that the TikTok GRWM trend has caused a massive, surging market of 7–18-year-old new skincare users that have no prior knowledge of the products they're using. This lack of education and accessibility is widespread, affecting not just teens but anyone, at any age, looking to enter their skincare journey.

When using multiple active ingredients at one time, there is a high risk of developing skin irritation, sun sensitivity and even atopic dermatitis. On average, TikTok’s top creators can be seen applying 11 or more products that feature almost 21 harsh active ingredients within 6-10 minutes – sometimes twice a day. Our recommendation to soothe this toxic trend: less is more. Simplifying your INCIs and focusing on fewer products with gentle, hydrating ingredients will serve all skin types and education levels. And with consumers forecasted to spend $48B on skincare with sensitive and soothing claims in 2025, we’ve got some space to fill in this category.
