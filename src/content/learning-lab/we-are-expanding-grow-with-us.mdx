When we expanded into skincare 10 years ago, we knew it would be an opportunity to grow our business. We knew our customers would be excited about this expansion, but we never imagined the rush of enthusiasm and explosion of growth that came our way. In a true show of the deep trust our customers and brand partners have placed in us, we have outgrown not one, but TWO skincare manufacturing facilities. So, now it’s time for an upgrade…

<PostImage source="/src/assets/images/hubfs/Williston-photos-2.jpg" alt="Williston photos (1)" width={252} align="right" />

in 2024, we acquired a new lease-to-purchase property in Williston, Vermont, which will become our new skincare manufacturing facility. This new space consists of two buildings on 37 acres of land and offers us 250,000 square feet of space – that’s more than double the footprint of our current skincare campus in Essex, Vermont! This new space provides us with a long runway for growth into the future.

For the last year, we’ve been hard at work preparing the new building. Over the course of the next year, we will be moving our Essex production operations into the new Williston building and adding some new equipment along the way. The move will be executed very slowly and thoughtfully to ensure that we do not interrupt production for our customers.

<PostImage source="/src/assets/images/hubfs/Williston-photos-2-1.jpg" alt="Williston photos (2)-1" width={256} align="left" />

In order to add capacity and new capabilities in our new space, we have invested in new equipment that will allow for smaller fill sizes, faster run times, and improved precision and flexibility.

As we continue to grow, we continue to expand our workforce. We have hired 150 workers over the last 18 months, and now proudly employ over 400 workers across our skincare and bar soap operations.

We’re excited to move into this new building and to continue to grow alongside our valued customers!
