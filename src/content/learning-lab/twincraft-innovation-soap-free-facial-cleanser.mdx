It has a bad reputation for potentially stripping the skin and adjusting the skin’s natural pH, but our team of innovators are experts at developing natural, gentle soap-based cleansers that will convert any soap-phobic user.

However, we do understand that there is market interest and demand for soap-free facial cleansers. Our Product Innovation Team developed a gentle Soap-Free Cleanser base to add to our growing library of cleanser bases, and the adaptable formula gives users a luxurious cleansing experience.
