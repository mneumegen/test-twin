Visiting Twincraft in Vermont: A Local Guide to Burlington

**Where to Stay**

Twincraft has facilities in Winooski and Williston, Vermont, both just a few miles from Burlington, with plenty of nearby hotels. Twincraft has special rates at a number of hotels, so contact your salesperson or account manager to learn more.

**Food**

Burlington's food scene continues to punch well above its weight for a small city. Here are some classic Burlington eateries:

- Breakfast

- - [Grey Jay](https://www.greyjayvt.com/) is a Mediterranean-inspired brunch spot located at the top of Church Street.  
  - [Meyers Bagels](https://www.myersbagels.com/?y_source=1_MjAwNjkwOTA4Ny03MTUtbG9jYXRpb24ud2Vic2l0ZQ%3D%3D) is a Montreal-inspired bagel and sandwich spot.

- Lunch

- - [Poppy's](https://www.poppyvt.com/) in the Old North End is a one-of-a-kind sandwich shop that features a weekly rotating menu.
  - [Rogue Rabbit](https://www.roguerabbitvt.com/) is a new lunch spot with classic focaccia pizzas and sandwiches.[](https://www.pizzeriaverita.com/)
  - [Zabby & Elf's Stone Soup](https://stonesoupvt.com/) is a Burlington staple featuring a fresh hot bar as well as legendary soups, sandwiches, and pastries. 
  - [Pingala](https://www.pingalacafe.com/) is a vegan, food truck-inspired, Guy Fieri-certified lunch spot that features their famous homemade crunchwrap supreme.
  - [August First](https://augustfirstvt.com/) is a coveted lunch spot, known for its homemade breads and mouthwatering sandwiches.

- Dinner

- - [Frankie's](https://www.frankiesvt.com/) on Cherry Street has quickly become one of Burlington's most exciting dinner spots, earning recognition as one of America's Top 10 Best New Restaurants. The menu changes daily and channels whatever's fresh from nearby farms and fishing boats. 
  - [Pizzeria Verita](https://www.pizzeriaverita.com/) is a mainstay in downtown Burlington for incredible wood-fired pizza.
  - [Honey Road](https://www.honeyroadrestaurant.com/) on Church Street Marketplace remains a go-to for creative Mediterranean flavors. 
  - [May Day](https://www.maydayvt.com/) in the Old North End is a rad spot that features seasonally inspired foods to pair with a stunning collection of cocktails and wines.
  - [Farmhouse Tap & Grill](https://burlington.farmhousetg.com/) remains a local institution for farm-to-table gastropub fare, with a gorgeous carved wood bar, a heated outdoor beer garden, and locally sourced meat from Vermont farms like Tamarack Farm and Vermont Heritage Grazers.

The maple creemee, Vermont's iconic take on soft serve, remains the quintessential summer treat, available at stands across the area. **Activities**

The Burlington Bike Path stretches 7.5 miles along Lake Champlain, with views opening up at every turn. Church Street Marketplace anchors downtown with pedestrian energy, local shops, patios, and street performers. Out on the water, the [Spirit of Ethan Allen](https://soea.com/) turns the lake into a front-row seat to the Adirondacks.[](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/things-to-do/)

The South End Arts District on Pine Street is home to local businesses and restaurants with a fun, artistic vibe, [](https://vermontvacation.com/places-to-visit/downtowns/burlington/)worth an afternoon wander. The [Burlington Farmers Market](https://burlingtonfarmersmarket.org/) on Saturday mornings i[](https://vermontvacation.com/places-to-visit/downtowns/burlington/)s also a fantastic way to experience local Vermont producers.

[ECHO](https://www.echovermont.org/), the science museum on the waterfront, is great for learning about Lake Champlain's ecosystem. [The Flynn Center for the Performing Arts](https://www.flynnvt.org/), an Art Deco landmark opened in 1930, is Burlington's cultural pulse, hosting everything from ballet to symphony to live music.[](https://travel.usnews.com/Burlington_VT/Things_To_Do/)

The [Vermont Comedy Club](https://www.vermontcomedyclub.com/) is a locally owned performing arts venue featuring standup, improv, and sketch comedy.[](https://vermontvacation.com/places-to-visit/downtowns/burlington/)

Head south to the [Shelburne Museum](https://shelburnemuseum.org/) for historical buildings, beautiful gardens, and a 220-foot steamboat. While in Shelburne, check out [Fiddlehead Brewing](https://fiddleheadbrewing.com/) and [Folino's Pizza,](https://www.folinopizza.com/) or stop by [Shelburne Vineyard](https://www.shelburnevineyard.com/) for a wine tasting.

**Breweries**

Vermont still leads the nation in craft breweries per capita. Top picks in the Burlington area include [Foam Brewers](https://www.foambrewers.com/), [Burlington Beer Company](https://www.burlingtonbeercompany.com/), [Zero Gravity,](https://www.zerogravitybeer.com/) and [Switchback](https://www.switchbackvt.com/). Visit the [Vermont Brewers Association](https://www.vermontbrewers.com/) for a full statewide list.

**Seasonal Activities**

Fall foliage through [Smuggler's Notch](https://www.smuggs.com/) is as spectacular as ever. Pair it with a stop at [Cold Hollow Cider Mill](https://coldhollow.com/?srsltid=AfmBOorr9qhkZlXmTppHlO33jmrsXSpzDLNgYupMkevyELZINkWiJTyH) for fresh cider donuts and a visit to [Cabot Creamery](https://cabotcreamery.com/?srsltid=AfmBOorlVTAaxupzhEQc0uGO76wNXi-VSWtFcJfxQ8rylbrQvPsZeql4) for all things cheese.

Winter visitors should consider [Stowe Mountain Resort](https://www.stowe.com/), [Sugarbush](https://www.sugarbush.com/), or [Bolton Valley](https://www.boltonvalley.com/) for skiing and snowboarding. In the summer, [Burlington Surf Club](https://www.burlingtonsurfclub.com/) offers paddleboard rentals, kitesurfing lessons, and open-air yoga on the waterfront. You can also catch a [Vermont Lake Monsters](https://vermontlakemonsters.com/) baseball game or a [Vermont Green FC](https://vermontgreenfc.com/) soccer match for some local sports flavor.

Whether you're touring our newest facilities or coming to see your latest pilot run, Vermont is waiting with open arms. Whatever the season, there are a million ways to soak up this Northeast flavor. We can't wait to see you!
