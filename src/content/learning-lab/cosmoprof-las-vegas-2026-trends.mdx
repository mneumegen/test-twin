Our team is finally back from our annual desert romp to Cosmoprof Las Vegas. Once again, the show did _not_ disappoint. While you'd need an entire week to see it in full, we're here to distill the show into the top trends for 2026. Here's your TL;DR on body, skin, and hair. Let's get into it:

#### Scalp-Focused Care

Hair care has risen from a niche category to a mainstream category in the beauty space. At Cosmoprof, scalp care was leading innovation in this space. Some of the top claims we saw in scalp care were hair longevity, sensorial textures, and active-ingredient stories. Scalp-specific products used to appear only as clinical solutions; now brands are differentiating themselves with luxurious, functional fragrances, innovative textures, and additional benefits that treat the cause rather than the symptom. Top consumers' concerns are reducing hair thinning and shedding, smoothing frizz, soothing dandruff, and scalp irritation. Scalp care is a lot like skincare and is beginning to borrow similar ingredient stories to address consumer concerns. This means brands that previously didn't offer hair care solutions can offer scalp-focused products as an extension of their skincare lines. 

#### Hair Styling 

At Cosmoprof, the buzz around hair care didn't end at the scalp. A 2026 Mintel study reveals that 43% of global consumers regularly use multiple hair-styling products, making customized regimens the new standard. We've also seen #HairProtection rack up an average of 1.3 million monthly views on TikTok.  While styling products are nothing new in the hair-care aisle, the real innovation at this year's show was the focus on increased hair strength, moisture, and elasticity. While hair strands are built to stretch up to 30% of their length, common styling practices can compromise their integrity over time, leading to breakage and irritation. Mintel also noted that consumers are starting to use treatment sprays not just for heat styling but also to protect against styling damage, environmental stressors, and to smooth frizz. Innovation for these marketing stories is led by hydrolyzed plant proteins, copper peptides, and botanical extracts. 

#### Skin Longevity

Per Mintel, the top three drivers of skincare purchases are multifunctional solutions, modern formulations, and additional skincare benefits. Cosmoprof '26 reflected this stat in troves, as booth after booth showcased collagen-boosting ingredients and NAD+-enriched formulas. Now that proactive "longevity" claims have successfully overtaken reactive "anti-aging" claims, consumers are becoming well-versed in ingredients that work _with_ your body to naturally produce collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid, rather than simply applying topically. 

#### UV Protection

SPF has become integral not only to consumers' routines but also to their anti-aging regimens. Per Mintel's 2026 sun care innovation report, 61% of US adults use SPF to address skin concerns beyond sun protection. At Cosmoprof, we saw UV filters (some SUPER new to the US market)  enter many new wearable textures with reparative and longevity claims. Skin-repairing claims, paired with soothing botanicals and cellular-repair enzymes, are driving innovation in multifunctional SPF products. Textures of note in this category include lightweight serums, cica-infused hot pours, gel with matte finish, and UV capsule technology.
